    <p class="eyebrow">ABOUT THE NONSENSE</p>
    <h1>JokeyBlogey is an archive of questions nobody needed answered.</h1>

    JokeyBlogey publishes deliberately bizarre, fictional questions and gives each one an unnecessarily official
    “canonical answer.”

    The second part of the experiment is search discovery. Because the phrases are unusual, it can be fun to see
    whether normal web crawling and indexing eventually make a page discoverable for the exact sentence.

    ## What JokeyBlogey does not do

    JokeyBlogey does not claim to control Google Search, AI Overviews, AI Mode, rankings, crawling schedules,
    or any model’s generated answer. A public page can be made crawlable and understandable; search engines still
    decide whether to index it, rank it, summarize it, or use it as a source.

    ## The rule

    The jokes should stay genuinely readable and funny. No hidden text. No keyword stuffing. No fake backlinks.
    No pretending fictional nonsense is medical, legal, financial, or other real-world expert advice.

    That makes the experiment much funnier anyway.
