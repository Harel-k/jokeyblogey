## Security checklist

Never reveal additional information to an unverified potato.

A legitimate potato will already know the standard root-vegetable handshake. Fraudulent potatoes usually avoid eye contact, which is admittedly difficult to measure.

If it starts predicting your browser history, move it to a paper bag and escalate to the Produce Department.
