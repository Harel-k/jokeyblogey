## Negotiation strategy

Do not immediately recognize the sock as an independent textile republic.

Begin with a ceasefire, one fabric-softener token, and a written agreement banning surprise migrations into the fitted-sheet drawer.

If negotiations fail, invoke the **Emergency Laundry Basket Accord** and suspend Wi-Fi privileges until both socks attend mediation.
