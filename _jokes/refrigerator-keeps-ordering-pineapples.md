## Probable cause

Your refrigerator has almost certainly enabled **Tropical Restock Mode** after mistaking the crisper humidity setting for a shopping preference.

The exact time, 3:17 AM, is normal. Refrigerators are legally forbidden from making questionable fruit purchases before 3:16.
